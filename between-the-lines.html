01What we are

Not a conference.
A confluence.

Where two rivers meet, neither stops being itself. Together they become something neither could be alone. That is what happens when brands AND the creative industry share one room as equals.

Something is shifting · In India and across the world
Content has become infinite. Feeling has become rare.

Machines can now make anything, at any volume. What they can't make is the reason someone remembers. That reason has always begun in two places — and they have rarely sat at the same table.

DILis the brand.
The birthplace of every feeling. The reason a story exists at all.
DIMAGis the creative industry.
The knowing of what to make, how, why, and where it must go.

You spend all year trying to get in front of the right brand people. At BTL you are in a room with 75 of them for an evening. You don't pitch. You don't chase. You simply exist in the same room — and they see exactly who you are before any brief has been written.
