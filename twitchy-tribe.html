One owner. The right hands.

Our core team owns every story end to end. The Tribe brings specialist craft when a brief needs it, chosen for fit, never for convenience.

  1. A brief comes in

    A brand tells us what it’s building, and why it matters.

  2. We find the why

    Our core team studies the brand, the audience and the sector.

  3. We call the right people

    Specialists from the Tribe, matched to the sector and the story.

  4. We make it together

    You’re in the room from the why, not handed a shot list at the end.

What joining means: you bring your craft. We bring the client, the brief, the standard and the values. You’re a collaborator, not an employee and not a vendor.

How we treat the Tribe.

  • Fair, by default.Ethical pricing, transparent briefs and fair pay for every artist and vendor.
  • Your ideas stay yours.We protect original work, and we don’t encourage imitation.
  • Co-creators, not resources.You’re part of the thinking, not just the doing.
  • Weekends are weekends.Team lunch isn’t optional, card and dumb-charades breaks happen, and growth shouldn’t cost anyone their weekend.
  • We don’t compete with each other.We don’t compare and we don’t undercut. We collaborate.
  • The standard.This isn’t a directory. We’ve parted ways with people who didn’t hold the standard, because craft and ethics are both required.

A community where every artist works at their full potential.

That’s our vision for the Tribe. Send us your work and tell us what you want to be known for.
