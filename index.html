Questions people ask us

What is Twitchy Thought Creations?

A curated creative collective in Mumbai, founded in 2018 by Geet Kumar. We create original IP, storytelling, social media and digital marketing, including films, events, podcasts and animation.

How does a creative collective work?

Our core team owns every story from start to finish. The Twitchy Tribe, 200+ vetted collaborators, adds specialist craft when a brief needs it, matched to your sector.

How is TTC different from a production house?

A production house executes a brief. We question it, research the brand, build a crew for that sector and stay accountable for whether the brand actually grew.

Who has TTC worked with?

NASSCOM, People Matters, UKG, Forbes India, Metfab Designs and Airowater, among 80+ brands. See the work page for the stories behind them.

How do I join the Twitchy Tribe?

Send your portfolio and the work you want to be known for through the Tribe form. We read every application, and if there’s a fit we’ll reach out.

Got a brief? Try us.
